We welcome all members, visitors, and future stewards of Our Church

At Christian Orthodox Church, our mission is to provide a sanctuary for worship, a space for spiritual growth, and a community that embodies the love and teachings of Jesus Christ. Rooted in the ancient traditions of the Eastern Orthodox Church, we strive to create an environment where individuals can experience the profound beauty of Orthodox Christian faith in a modern context.
